# Gemini Project Instructions

Read `AGENTS.md` first.

Follow its AI tracking workflow at `docs/ai/README.md`: read `CONTEXT.md` and
`TASKS.md` before work, then record actual progress and handoff in the project.
These records are portable across computers; they are not execution authorization.

For any UI, visual, frontend, Expo, KMP Android, or KMP Desktop work, also read
`docs/design/MND_STITCH_AI_RULES.md` before proposing or editing. The current
design authority is Stitch project `projects/13275961100622290348` and its local
mirror under `.stitch` and `web/assets/stitch-remote`.

Do not invent a new UI direction. You may give clearly labeled suggestions, but
implementation must follow the existing Honey, Mint, Night, and Neutral Stitch
V2 rules unless the user explicitly approves a deviation.
